**Short answer:** 
Zyada tar states mein landlord ko vacant possession milte hi security deposit wapas dena hota hai, sirf actual dues katne ke baad [1]. Isliye aapke paas strong case lagta hai, lekin ye Model Tenancy Act sirf un states mein lagu hota hai jinhone apna tenancy law adopt kiya ho – Maharashtra ka status mujhe confirm nahi hai. 

**Aapke options (easy se hard):**

1. **Written demand (demand letter)** 
 - Ek simple patra likho: “15,000 Rs ka advance deposit wapas karo, 7 din ke andar nahi mila to legal action lunga.” 
 - Cost: free (postage ya courier). 
 - Jo bhi proof hai (bank statement, chat, WhatsApp) usko attach karo. 
 - Ye pehla step hota hai, court bhi isse dekh sakta hai.

2. **Free legal‑aid & advice** 
 - NALSA ka helpline 15100 call karo; agar aapki income limit ke andar hain to free lawyer mil sakta hai [2]. 
 - Unko apni situation aur jo documents hai (bank statement, chat) dikhaye.

3. **Lok Adalat (people’s court) settlement** 
 - Dono parties ko agree karna hota hai. Agar owner maan le to aap dono milke settlement kar sakte hain; decision final hota hai, appeal nahi hoti [3]. 
 - Cost: usually zero ya nominal. 
 - Aapko written demand aur proof dikhana padega.

4. **Summary suit under CPC Order XXXVII** 
 - Agar aapke paas likhit rent agreement ya receipt hai, to ek “summary suit” file kar sakte hain – ye fast civil suit hota hai, dusri party ko sirf limited defence milti hai [4]. 
 - Cost: lawyer fees (agar free legal aid mil jaye to kam). 
 - Court ka process 2‑3 mahine tak chal sakta hai.

5. **Ordinary civil suit for money recovery** 
 - General suit file karna padta hai, 3 saal ka limitation period hai – aapka right aaj se 3 saal ke andar hi file karna hoga [5]. 
 - Cost: court fees + lawyer fees. 
 - Time: 6‑12 mahine ya usse zyada.

**Important deadline:** 
Deposit recovery ke liye suit file karne ka time limit 3 saal hai, to jaldi action lein [5].

**Jo documents chahiye:** 
- Bank statement ya receipt showing Rs 15,000 advance. 
- Lease agreement ya koi likhit notice of tenancy (agar hai). 
- Owner ke saath ki gayi messages/WhatsApp chats jisme deposit ki baat ho.

**Mujhe sure nahi hai:** 
- Maharashtra ne Model Tenancy Act adopt kiya hai ya nahi, isliye exact statutory right confirm nahi kar sakta. 
- Owner ke against koi specific consumer‑forum jurisdiction hai ya nahi.

**Lawyer ki zarurat:** 
Agar owner demand letter ka jawab nahi deta, ya aapko likhit agreement/receipt nahi mil raha, to ek lawyer se milna behtar rahega. Free legal aid ke liye 15100 call kar sakte hain. 

*Main lawyer nahi hoon, bas basic guidance de raha hoon.*
