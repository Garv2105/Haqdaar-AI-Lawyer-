**Short answer** 
Yes, the law is on your side. Not refunding a returned product is a “deficiency” in service and an “unfair trade practice” under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 [1][2]. The e‑commerce platform also had to resolve your grievance within one month, but it has not done so [3].

**What you can do – from easiest to hardest**

1. **Send a final written demand** 
 *What:* A polite but firm letter (email or registered post) asking for the Rs 8,499 refund within 7 days, citing the above provisions. 
 *Cost:* Free (post fee only). 
 *Need:* Order number, name of the store, proof of pickup (delivery receipt). 

2. **Escalate to the grievance officer again** 
 *What:* Call or email the officer, remind them of the 48‑hour acknowledgment rule and the one‑month resolution deadline [3]. Mention you will file a consumer complaint if no refund is received. 
 *Cost:* Free. 
 *Need:* Same documents as above plus a copy of your earlier email. 

3. **File a consumer complaint** 
 *What:* Lodge a complaint with the District Consumer Commission (Jaipur) – the value (Rs 8,499) is well within its jurisdiction [4]. Use the e‑Jagriti portal (the online filing system) [5]. 
 *Cost:* No filing fee for claims up to Rs 5 lakh [6]. 
 *Time limit:* You must file within 2 years from the date the problem arose (the refund date) [7]. 
 *Need:* Order number, store name, copy of pickup confirmation, copies of all communications, and your demand letter. 

4. **Seek help from consumer helplines** 
 *National Consumer Helpline:* 1915 or 1800‑11‑4000. 
 *Free legal‑aid helpline (NALSA):* 15100. They can guide you on drafting the demand and filing the complaint. 

**Deadlines to watch** 
- The platform should have resolved the grievance within one month of your complaint (by early October). That deadline has passed, strengthening your case. 
- You have until **20 September 2028** to file a consumer complaint (2 years from the return date) [7].

**What I’m not sure about** 
- The exact name of the online store and the order number – you’ll need these for the complaint. 
- Whether the platform gave you any written promise of a specific refund timeline; if not, the 30‑day rule in the Act applies [2].

**When to see a lawyer** 
If the platform still refuses after the consumer commission’s order, or if you feel the process is too complex, a lawyer can help enforce the order. You can get free assistance through the NALSA helpline (15100). 

Good luck, and keep all your documents together!
